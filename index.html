PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: 28 October 2026

1. About this prototype

PokeDuel is a fan-made, non-commercial Pokémon battle game prototype. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to Nintendo, The Pokémon Company, Game Freak, or any official Pokémon product.

2. Information we store

When signed out, your team, coins, items, and progress are stored in this browser's local storage. When you sign in with Google, PokeDuel uses Firebase Authentication for sign-in and stores your game save plus your Google display name, email address, and profile-photo URL in a private Firestore document associated with your Firebase user ID. The app is designed to let each account access only its own save. Signing out does not erase this browser's local copy. Clearing browser site data may erase the local copy; cloud data remains until it is deleted under the project's account-data process.

3. Third-party services

The game may request species and sprite data from PokéAPI and its public sprite repository. Those services may receive standard connection information, such as your IP address, when your browser requests assets. Their own privacy terms apply. Battle backgrounds in this prototype are original CSS-generated scenery; external images may be added only where the source and usage rights permit.

4. Google sign-in

Google sign-in is provided by Firebase Authentication. Game progress and account display metadata are stored in Firebase Cloud Firestore. This prototype does not expose public profiles or chat. A self-service cloud-account deletion flow is not yet available; before a public launch, the operator must provide a clear way to request deletion and review the applicable privacy requirements.

5. Children and accounts

Do not submit sensitive personal information. No user account, chat, or public profile service is active in this prototype.

6. Changes and contact

This notice may change as the project develops. No dedicated support email has been configured for this prototype.

This starter policy is informational and is not legal advice. Review it and replace placeholders with actual service, operator, and contact details before public launch.
