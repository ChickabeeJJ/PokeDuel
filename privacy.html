Privacy policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

1. About PokeDuel

PokeDuel is an unofficial, non-commercial fan-made Pokémon battle game. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to Nintendo, The Pokémon Company, Game Freak, or any official Pokémon product. There are no real-money purchases.

2. Information stored on your device

Your team, coins, items and progress are stored in this browser's local storage. A backup copy may also be kept there when you import, reset or upgrade a save. Clearing site data removes these copies. Save files or save codes that you export stay wherever you choose to keep them.

2a. Save protection

To keep leaderboards fair, saves carry a tamper seal and a record of in-game earnings. This information never leaves your device unless you sign in or export. Exported saves are encrypted with a password you choose. PokeDuel never sees or stores that password, and a forgotten password can't be recovered. If a save fails its integrity checks, it stays playable but its leaderboard entry is removed.

3. Google sign-in and cloud saves

If you sign in with Google, Firebase Authentication processes the sign-in. PokeDuel stores your game save, your Google display name, email address and profile-photo URL in a Firestore document linked to your Firebase user ID. Each account is restricted to its own save document.

4. Leaderboards

When you are signed in, PokeDuel publishes a leaderboard entry that other signed-in players can see. It contains:

  • your chosen trainer name (not your Google name or email);
  • the Pokémon species you picked as an avatar;
  • your Trainer Score, wins, Pokédex count, shiny count, best check-in streak and recent daily scores;
  • up to three showcased Pokémon (species, nickname, level, shiny, mark, gender, nature, ability, held item, Tera Type, IVs, EVs and moves).

Please don't use personal information as your trainer name or Pokémon nicknames.

Friends. Your friend code is the first 10 characters of your account's internal ID (not your email). Anyone who has it can find your leaderboard entry. Your friends list is stored in your save, so it syncs with your cloud save. Adding someone doesn't notify them or share anything beyond your public leaderboard entry.

5. Third-party services

When your browser loads data or images, these providers may receive ordinary connection information, such as your IP address. Their own terms and privacy notices apply.

  • Pokémon sprites, artwork and item icons come from the public PokéAPI sprite repository, hosted on GitHub.
  • Pokédex text comes from PokéAPI.
  • The Barlow font comes from Google Fonts.
  • The sign-in and save libraries come from Google's Firebase CDN.

6. Account deletion

Self-service deletion of cloud saves and leaderboard entries is not available yet. Before a public launch, the operator must provide a clear way to request deletion.

7. Children and sensitive information

Don't enter sensitive personal information anywhere in the game. PokeDuel has no chat or messaging.

8. Changes and contact

This notice may change as the project develops. A dedicated support contact has not been set up yet.

This notice is provided for product development and is not legal advice. Before a public launch, add the operator's contact details, verify every service listed and review the applicable legal requirements.
