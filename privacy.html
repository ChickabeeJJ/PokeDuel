PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: 28 October 2026

1. About this prototype

PokeDuel is an unofficial fan-made Pokémon battle simulator prototype. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to Nintendo, The Pokémon Company, Game Freak, or any official Pokémon product.

2. Information stored

When signed out, team data, coins, items, and progress are stored in the current browser's local storage. When you sign in with Google, Firebase Authentication processes the sign-in, and PokeDuel stores the game save plus your Google display name, email address, and profile-photo URL in a Firestore document associated with your Firebase user ID. The app is intended to restrict each user to their own save. Signing out does not erase the local copy. Clearing browser site data may remove that local copy, while cloud data remains until deleted through the project's account-data process.

3. Third-party services

The game requests Pokémon species and sprite information from PokéAPI and its publicly available sprite repository. Those providers may receive ordinary connection information, including your IP address, when your browser requests data or images. Their own terms and privacy notices apply. The current battle backgrounds are CSS-generated; third-party artwork should only be added when the relevant usage rights permit it.

4. Google sign-in and accounts

Google sign-in is provided by Firebase Authentication. Game progress and account display metadata are stored in Firebase Cloud Firestore. This prototype has no public profiles or chat. A self-service account deletion flow is not yet available; before public launch, the operator must provide a clear process for requesting deletion and review applicable privacy requirements.

5. Children and sensitive information

Do not submit sensitive personal information. This prototype has no active account service, public profile system, or chat feature.

6. Policy updates and contact

This notice may be revised as the project changes. A dedicated support contact has not yet been configured.

This starter notice is provided for product development and is not legal advice. Before a public launch, replace the missing operator/contact details, verify all actual services, and review any applicable legal requirements.
